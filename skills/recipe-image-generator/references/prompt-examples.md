- Angle: 45-degree
- Lighting: warm natural from left
- Surface: dark wood table
- Plate: white ceramic bowl
- Mood: homey, comforting

```
Professional food photography of Mapo Tofu, silky white tofu cubes in a rich red-brown spicy sauce with ground pork, topped with chopped scallions and Sichuan peppercorn.
Shot from 45-degree angle. Served in a white ceramic bowl on a dark wood table.
Warm natural lighting from the left side. Shallow depth of field.
Steam rising from the hot dish. Vibrant red chili oil glistening on surface.
A pair of wooden chopsticks resting on the side. Small rice bowl slightly blurred in background.
High-end food magazine quality, 4K resolution.
```

## Example 4: Dumpling Serving Shot

**Reference image analysis:**
- Angle: eye level, slightly above
- Lighting: soft window light from right
- Surface: bamboo steamer on wooden table
- Props: dipping sauce, chopsticks
- Mood: traditional, authentic

**Generated prompt:**
```
Professional food photography of Chinese steamed dumplings (jiaozi) in a traditional bamboo steamer.
6 perfectly pleated dumplings with translucent wrapper showing filling inside.
Shot from slightly above eye level. Bamboo steamer on a natural wood table.
Soft window light from the right side. Gentle steam rising from the dumplings.
Small dish of black vinegar with julienned ginger beside the steamer.
Wooden chopsticks picking up one dumpling. Warm, inviting atmosphere.
Traditional Chinese home cooking style, high quality food photography.
```

## Style Modifiers

Add these to any prompt for specific effects:

| Effect | Modifier |
|--------|----------|
| More rustic | "on a weathered wood surface, linen napkin, imperfect styling" |
| More modern | "minimalist plating, geometric composition, clean white space" |
| More dramatic | "dark moody lighting, high contrast, single directional light" |
| More bright | "high-key lighting, airy and bright, white background elements" |
| Chinese restaurant | "red lacquer table, patterned porcelain, traditional Chinese dining setting" |
| Street food | "market stall backdrop, casual paper wrapping, bustling atmosphere" |
